VoltProof

An independent battery-health report for used electric scooters, so a buyer and a dealer can agree a fair price.

PROTOTYPE · DEMO DATA
Step 1

Dealer books a check for a used e-scooter.

Step 2

Inspector does a full charge-and-ride test.

Step 3

Readings go in below; the report is generated.

Step 4

The report and QR code go to the buyer.

How the band is set: new price × age retention × battery-health factor, ±6%. The year-3 factor (0.57) is the midpoint of Ampere's 51–63% estimate; the other factors are VoltProof assumptions still to be checked against real dealer prices. The QR holds demo text, not a live record.

VoltProof is a student startup prototype for MDMIE03 (BE Presentation 2). Price and battery figures are published estimates: battery replacement costs from EVFY (Apr 2026); resale retention from Ampere (May 2026).
